        What LightMoon has learned

          How it works

          Each time you finish, snooze or skip a reminder, LightMoon notes how it went. After a few of the same kind it spots patterns, like something you always do 20 minutes late, a reminder you keep snoozing, or the hours you get things done fastest. It uses them to suggest better times; it never moves a reminder on its own. It syncs with your other devices.

          Accountability buddy

          How it works

          Someone who cheers you on. After your Sunday letter, LightMoon offers to send them a two-line note about your week from the LightMoon app on your phone. It always shows you the exact words first and sends only if you say yes. Nothing else about you is shared. Leave the name empty to switch it off.

          Speaking

          Listening

          Wake word

          How it works

          With the app closed, say "LightMoon", wait for the chime, then speak. It opens ready to listen; on a locked phone it asks for your fingerprint first. It listens only for its name, entirely on this phone (the first time, it downloads a 41 MB offline voice model). It uses some battery, and Android shows a microphone dot while it listens.

          Advanced

          Offline brain

          How it works

          A small AI that runs on this phone itself, so LightMoon still answers with no internet: setting reminders, ticking tasks off, remembering things and simple questions. It's much less clever than the main AI, which takes over again as soon as you're back online. It only downloads once, and nothing you say to it leaves the phone.

          Our own model

          How it works

          While this is on, LightMoon keeps a copy of each answer the big AI gives, here on this device, so a small AI of our own can learn to answer the same way. It leaves out the phone's calls, messages and calendar, and anything that looks like a one-time code or password. Nothing leaves this device unless you download the file.

          To teach it, upload the file to a free Kaggle notebook and press Run (trainer/README.md in the LightMoon project has the steps).

          Our model on this computer

          How it works

          After each answer from the big AI, LightMoon quietly asks our model the same thing and checks whether it would have done the same: the same tool with the same details, or a reply that says much the same. You never see or hear its answers.

          With the switch on, once it agrees at least 95 times in 100 over at least 50 questions of one kind, it answers that kind itself. If it has any trouble, the big AI answers instead. If Edge asks to let LightMoon reach devices on your network, allow it: that's our model, on this computer.

          Checking in

          How it works

          When a reminder needs doing, LightMoon checks back until you tell it it's done, say "later" (it asks again in 10 minutes) or say "stop asking". You can answer out loud, with the buttons under the reminder, or with Done and Snooze on the notification. It never checks in between 11 pm and 7 am, and not for a plain heads-up like a meeting starting.

          Reminder notifications

          How it works

          Reminders pop up on this device even when LightMoon is closed. The LightMoon cloud only learns when each reminder is due, never what it says.

          On Android, the LightMoon app also says each reminder out loud at its time, even with the phone locked.

          Spoken reminders

          How it works

          The phone says each reminder out loud at its time, even when LightMoon is closed or the screen is off, at alarm volume. With sync on, it also picks up reminders set on your other devices (it checks about every 15 minutes).

          Weather

          Privacy

          Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, free. Only your city's rough location is sent.

          Weekly letter

          What it says

          A few short paragraphs in the chat, from 6 pm on Sundays: what went well, what slipped, a pattern it noticed and one suggestion for the week ahead. Reply to correct anything it got wrong, and it remembers.

          Spoken briefings

          What it says

          The briefings are said at alarm volume, even with LightMoon closed: the weather (for your city above), meetings, reminders, and in the morning the calls and messages you missed overnight.

          "Reading messages" is greyed out?

          If Android greys out LightMoon on the notification access screen ("restricted setting"), open phone Settings → Apps → LightMoon → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, then try again. Android does this for apps installed outside the Play Store.

          Say out loud

          What else it can do on the phone

          You can ask LightMoon about your meetings, missed calls and messages, and have it call or text people, reply on WhatsApp or SMS, add meetings, open apps, play or skip music, navigate, search, set alarms and timers, and work the flashlight, volume and ringer. It always reads back and waits for your yes before calling or sending anything. Calls and messages are only spoken when the phone isn't on silent.

          When you talk to LightMoon, your upcoming meetings, recent calls and the last few messages go to the AI service along with the conversation.

          Apps

          How it works

          An app switched off is ignored completely: LightMoon doesn't keep, say or share anything from it, and forgets what it had. Apps appear here after their first message or email arrives.

          Email accounts

          How it works

          LightMoon reads only the accounts switched on here, and new accounts start off. It sees each new email as it arrives (who it's from, the subject and the first lines), not your older inbox, and it never reads emails out loud.

          Chats and people

          How it works

          Switch off a chat, group or person and LightMoon never reads it again, and forgets what it had from them. A person switched off is skipped in groups too. The 40 most recent chats are listed.

          Sync between devices

          Scan it with LightMoon or Life Levelling on your phone. Anyone who scans it can read your LightMoon data, so only show it to your own devices.

          Point the camera at the QR code on your other device.

          How it works

          On your other device, open Settings → Sync and Life Levelling and scan this device's QR code, or choose I have a code and type it: both devices' memories, tasks, goals and chats are combined. Already syncing with a different code? Use another code switches this device over and brings what it has along. Everything is encrypted with the code before it leaves the device, so keep the code private. Your API key, PIN and voice settings stay on each device.

          Life Levelling

          App lock

          How it works

          Asks for your PIN or fingerprint when the app opens and after a few minutes away. It keeps others out of the app; it doesn't encrypt your data.

          Your data

          Everything is stored only on this device. Backups don't include your API key.
