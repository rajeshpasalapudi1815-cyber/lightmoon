        Settings

        AI brain (advanced)
        Weather in briefings Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, free. Only your city's rough location is sent.
        Reminder notifications: Reminders pop up on this device even when LightMoon is closed. The LightMoon cloud only learns when each reminder is due, never what it says. On Android, the LightMoon app also says each reminder out loud at its time, even with the phone locked.
        Spoken reminders: The phone says each reminder out loud at its time, even when LightMoon is closed or the screen is off, at alarm volume. With sync on, it also picks up reminders set on your other devices (it checks about every 15 minutes).
        Wake word: With the app closed, say "LightMoon", wait for the chime, then speak. It opens ready to listen; on a locked phone it asks for your fingerprint first. It listens only for its name, entirely on this phone (the first time, it downloads a 41 MB offline voice model). It uses some battery, and Android shows a microphone dot while it listens.
        Phone: The briefings are said at alarm volume, even with LightMoon closed: the weather (for your weather place), meetings, reminders, and in the morning the calls and messages you missed overnight. Then you can ask LightMoon about your meetings, missed calls and messages, and have it call or text people, reply on WhatsApp or SMS, add meetings, open apps, play or skip music, navigate, search, set alarms and timers, and work the flashlight, volume and ringer. It always reads back and waits for your yes before calling or sending anything. Calls and messages are only spoken when the phone isn't on silent. When you talk to LightMoon, your upcoming meetings, recent calls and the last few messages go to the AI service along with the conversation.
        Sync between devices:
        On your other device, open Settings → I have a code and enter this code: both devices' memories, tasks, goals and chats are combined. Everything is encrypted with the code before it leaves the device, so keep the code private. Your API key, PIN and voice settings stay on each device. Life Levelling:
        App lock:
        Asks for your PIN or fingerprint when the app opens and after a few minutes away. It keeps others out of the app; it doesn't encrypt your data.
        Your data Everything is stored only on this device. Backups don't include your API key.
